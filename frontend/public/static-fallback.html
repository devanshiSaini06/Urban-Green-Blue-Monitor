📊 Key Caveats (Read Before Interpreting)

  1. 2018 data quality: Sentinel-2 coverage over India was thinner in 2018; fewer images, lower coverage.
  2. Built-up ≠ impervious: Spectral NDBI class captures bare soil and fallow fields; use Dynamic World classifier for comparison.
  3. Seasonal window: Oct–Dec mixes post-monsoon water and crop residues; compare with other seasons.
  4. Boundary dilution: Districts include large rural areas, diluting urban change signal.
  5. Low trend power: n=8 years gives very low statistical power for trend tests.
  6. Validation limited: WorldCover reference only for 2020/2021.
  7. Threshold sensitivity: Results sensitive to NDVI/MNDWI/NDBI thresholds.
